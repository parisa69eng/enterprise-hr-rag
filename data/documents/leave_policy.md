# Leave Policy

Document ID: HR-001
Version: 1.0

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Public holidays are not included in this allowance.

Employees should submit leave requests through the HR portal
at least five working days before the planned leave date.

Managers review requests based on team capacity and business needs.

## Sick Leave

Employees who cannot work because of illness should notify their
manager as soon as reasonably possible.

For absences lasting more than three consecutive working days,
employees must provide a medical certificate.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to five unused annual leave days
into the following calendar year.

Carried-over days must be used by March 31.
Unused carried-over days expire after this date.
