# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: HR-003
Version: 1.0

## Eligible Expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business travel,
business meals, and work-related supplies.

Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.

## Submission Deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days
of the expense date.

## Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for individual expenses above €25.

For expenses of €25 or less, employees should retain receipts
when available and provide a clear description of the expense.

## Approval

All expense claims require manager approval.

Claims that do not meet the policy requirements may be rejected.
