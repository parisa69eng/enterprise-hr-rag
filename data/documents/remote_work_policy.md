# Remote Work Policy

Document ID: HR-002
Version: 1.0

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may request
remote work, subject to manager approval.

## Remote Work Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

Teams may establish specific in-office days for collaboration
and operational requirements.

## Equipment

The company provides an approved laptop for remote work.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure working
environment and protecting company information.

## Approval

Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR portal.
The manager must approve the request before the arrangement begins.
